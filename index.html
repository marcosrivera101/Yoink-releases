How to Install

Tap Download for AndroidChrome may say the file could be harmful. That's what it says for every app that isn't from the Play Store. Tap Download anyway.
Open the fileTap the download when it finishes, or find Yoink-64bit.apk in your Files app under Downloads.
Allow the installAndroid asks to allow installs from Chrome (or Files). Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
Tap Install, then OpenThat's it. Yoink updates itself from inside the app after this (⋮ → Check for updates).
